Mark Bench Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Mark Bench is a logo design tool. It does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

  • The logos you design stay on your device. Your last design is remembered in your device's local app storage so it is there when you return.
  • Files you save (PNG or SVG) go to your device only.
  • The app loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive your device's IP address as part of that request, under Google's privacy policy.
  • The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.
